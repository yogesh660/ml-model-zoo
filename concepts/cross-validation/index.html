Cross-Validation & Resampling Strategies

Data hygiene, out-of-fold estimation, stratified resampling, and leak-free model evaluation strategies.

1. The Core Resampling Methodologies

Strategy 01

k-Fold Cross-Validation

Partitions training data into $k$ equal subsets. The model trains on $k-1$ folds and tests on the remaining fold, repeating $k$ times to produce an unbiased mean metric.

Strategy 02

Stratified k-Fold

Preserves the exact percentage of target classes in every fold. Crucial for imbalanced datasets (e.g. Titanic survival rate or medical diagnosis).

Strategy 03

Time-Series Split

Enforces strict temporal ordering ($t < t+1$) using rolling or expanding windows, strictly preventing look-ahead data leakage.
