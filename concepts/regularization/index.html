Regularization Techniques: L1, L2 & Elastic Net

Constrained parameter shrinkage dynamics: geometry of L1 diamond sparsity versus L2 spherical weight decay.

1. L1 Lasso vs. L2 Ridge Geometry

Unconstrained empirical risk minimization often yields large, unstable coefficients. Regularization introduces a constraint ball on the parameter space:

Regularizer Penalty Term $\Omega(\beta)$ Constraint Geometry Sparsity Bayesian Prior Interpretation
Ridge (L2 / Tikhonov) \lambda \sum \beta_j^2 Hypersphere / Smooth Circle No (shrinks weights toward 0 asymptotically) Gaussian Prior $\beta_j \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \tau^2)$
Lasso (L1) \lambda \sum |\beta_j| Diamond / Polytope with sharp corners Yes (forces weights strictly to 0.0) Laplace Prior $\beta_j \sim \text{Laplace}(0, b)$
Elastic Net \lambda_1 \|\beta\|_1 + \lambda_2 \|\beta\|_2^2 Convex combination of diamond and circle Selective (groups correlated predictors) Mixed Gaussian-Laplace prior
