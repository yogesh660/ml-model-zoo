1. Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) Mechanics

The ROC curve plots the True Positive Rate (Sensitivity) against the False Positive Rate (1 - Specificity) across every possible classification threshold $\tau \in [0, 1]$:

$$\text{TPR}(\tau) = \frac{\text{TP}(\tau)}{\text{TP}(\tau) + \text{FN}(\tau)}, \quad \text{FPR}(\tau) = \frac{\text{FP}(\tau)}{\text{FP}(\tau) + \text{TN}(\tau)}$$
Probabilistic Interpretation of AUC

The Area Under the ROC Curve (AUC) has an elegant statistical interpretation: it is the exact probability that the classifier will rank a randomly chosen positive instance higher than a randomly chosen negative instance:

$$\text{AUC} = P\left( f(x^+) > f(x^-) \right)$$
  • $\text{AUC} = 1.0$: Perfect separable classification.
  • $\text{AUC} = 0.5$: Diagonal line, equivalent to random coin tossing.
  • $\text{AUC} < 0.5$: Worse than random (invert predictions to obtain $1 - \text{AUC}$).
