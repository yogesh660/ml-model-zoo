1. The 4 Pillars of Production EDA

Pillar 1 / Univariate

Distribution & Skewness

Examine mean, median, IQR, and skewness coefficients. Features with skewness $> 1.0$ (e.g. income or prices) benefit from logarithmic or Box-Cox power transforms.

Pillar 2 / Multivariate

Correlation Matrices

Compute Pearson $r$ for linear interactions and Spearman $\rho$ for monotonic rank associations to identify predictive features and flag multicollinearity.

Pillar 3 / Data Hygiene

Missingness Mechanisms

Differentiate Missing Completely at Random (MCAR), Missing at Random (MAR), and Missing Not at Random (MNAR) to apply appropriate imputation pipelines.

Pillar 4 / Robustness

Outlier Detection

Isolate high-leverage anomalies using Tukey's Interquartile Range ($1.5 \times \text{IQR}$) or Z-score criteria ($|z| > 3.0$).
