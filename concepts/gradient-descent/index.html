Gradient Descent & Numerical Optimization

Iterative first-order optimization algorithms: Batch, Stochastic, Mini-batch, Momentum, RMSprop, and Adam.

1. The Update Rule & Variants

For parameterized model with loss $J(\theta)$, gradient descent updates parameters along the negative direction of steepest gradient:

$$\theta_{t+1} = \theta_t - \eta \nabla_\theta J(\theta_t)$$

Where $\eta > 0$ represents the learning rate.
