02 / Empirical Verification

Modular benchmark datasets.

No model is an island. Switch real-world datasets inside model pages to inspect EDA transformations, collinearity, and feature selection choices.

Continuous Regression 20,640 Records

California Housing

Predicting median house values from spatial coordinates, census block demographics, and room distributions.

Key EDA: Right-skewed income target, spatial latitude/longitude clusters. Feature Selection: VIF drops redundant AveBedrms collinearity with AveRooms. Evaluated On: OLS, Ridge, Lasso, ElasticNet, SVR, Decision Trees, XGBoost.
Binary Classification 891 Records

Titanic Passenger Survival

Binary outcome survival prediction modeling socioeconomic status, passenger demographics, and family size.

Key EDA: Missing age median imputation, bimodal age curves, title extraction. Feature Selection: Mutual information isolates Sex and Pclass as dominant factors. Evaluated On: Logistic Regression, LDA, QDA, Naive Bayes, Random Forest.
High-Dim Classification 569 Records • 30 Features

Breast Cancer Wisconsin

Cell nucleus characteristics digitized from fine needle aspirate (FNA) images diagnosing malignant vs benign tissue.

Key EDA: Strong geometric correlation between radius, perimeter, and area. Feature Selection: Standard scaling and correlation thresholding eliminate redundant variance. Evaluated On: SVM (Linear & RBF), MLP Classifier, KNN, Logistic Regression.
Unsupervised & Multi-Class 178 - 1,599 Records

Wine Quality & Customer Segments

Physicochemical compositions and customer behavioral spending profiles for density geometry benchmarking.

Key EDA: Varying attribute scales requiring z-score normalization and PCA projections. Feature Selection: Silhouette score maximization and Elbow method (WCSS). Evaluated On: K-Means, DBSCAN, Agglomerative Hierarchical, GMM, Mean Shift.
